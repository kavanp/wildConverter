Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At WildConverter, we take your privacy seriously. We've built our service with privacy as a core principle. No signup, no tracking, no data collection.

Information We Don't Collect

Unlike many online services, WildConverter does NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials (we don't have accounts!)
  • Payment information (our service is 100% free)
  • Location data
  • Device fingerprints for tracking

File Handling

When you upload a file for conversion:

  • Temporary Processing: Your file is temporarily stored in server memory only during the conversion process.
  • Immediate Deletion: Files are automatically deleted immediately after conversion is complete.
  • No Storage: We do not store, archive, or backup your files.
  • No Access: Our team does not access or view your files.

Cookies

WildConverter uses minimal cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Only for basic website functionality.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We don't use cookies to track your activity across the web.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: To display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Content Delivery Networks: To serve static files quickly and securely.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transfers
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • No persistent storage of user files

Children's Privacy

WildConverter is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website.
